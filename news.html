Gallery system is now live!

15 / 05 / 2025

The community asset gallery is officially open to everyone. From today, any registered user can upload their own sprites, backgrounds, enemies and items to be featured in the game.

Assets are reviewed by the community through the voting system — the more votes an asset gets, the more likely it is to appear in future runs. This means the game literally gets shaped by the players.

Head over to the gallery section, browse what other players have already uploaded, and submit your own creations. The best assets will make it into the next build!
